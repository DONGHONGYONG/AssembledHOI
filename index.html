AssembledHOI

Compositional Synthesis of 3D Human-Object Interaction via Assembling Gaussian Priors

Abstract

Recent advances in 3D generation have enabled zero-shot text-to-HOI (Human-Object Interaction) synthesis. However, existing methods either lack explicit modeling of human-object spatial relationships or fail to consider collaborative optimization of geometry and texture. To address these issues, we propose AssembledHOI, a novel framework that achieves efficient and accurate 3D HOI synthesis through structured assembly of Gaussian priors for humans and objects. Specifically, rather than treating the scene as a monolithic entity throughout the entire process, our approach explicitly decouples the human and the object components at initialization and gets the priors of body pose and object through existing 3D generation models. Moreover, we introduce part-aware interaction optimization that achieves semantically aligned human-object position configuration by modulating Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) gradients on interaction-relevant body parts. In addition, we design part-guided textural enhancement to refine interaction regions via localized supervision, thereby improving texture fidelity while reinforcing positional consistency. Quantitative experiments demonstrate that AssembledHOI attains state-of-the-art CLIP scores, with a running time of 25 minutes, over 3 × faster than existing methods.

Qualitative Comparison

When given complex human-object interaction prompts, such as “a man in a hoodie, camouflage jacket, joggers and trainers carrying a backpack”, methods like DreamFusion, Magic3D and TextMesh produced results with insufficient physical plausibility, exhibiting noticeable structural distortions. Although InterFusion and InteractAnything generate geometrically coherent human-object interactions, like the others, it suffers from textures that lack richness and visual fidelity compared to our approach. Furthermore, while DreamHOI relies on UV maps to generate diverse styles, our approach eliminates this dependency. In terms of text prompt alignment, our method also demonstrates clear superiority over all six baseline approaches.

Ablation Study

The results show that AAS alone achieves a significant improvement of +6.36% in the CLIP score, highlighting its critical role in selecting semantically meaningful anchors for human-object interaction. When combined with PALR, the performance increases further to 0.3652, indicating that local refinement improves fine-grained alignment between body parts and objects. Finally, integrating PGTE leads to a further boost of +1.83%, confirming its effectiveness in enriching texture details by the part-guided mechanism. Notably, the full model achieves the highest CLIP score (0.3835), the highest R-Contact (53.7%) and the lowest R-Collision (13.6%), surpassing all individual or partial combinations. This consistent improvement across components demonstrates the synergistic effect among AAS, PALR and PGTE.

Extension

Loading extension videos…

Since the core of our method lies in the assembly of humans and objects, AssembledHOI can be naturally extended to enable zero-shot generation of human-human interactions. Our method achieves generalization by disentangling structural components, e.g. pose from an object, allowing the text-guided assembly of novel interactions among unseen characters. As shown in above, we generate scenes of interactions such as “Batman and Iron Man shaking hands” or “Spider-Man and Captain America waving”. These results demonstrate the strong compositional generalization ability of our framework, where new interactions are synthesized by recombining learned components based on text prompts.
